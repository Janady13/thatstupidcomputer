## Practice brief

The professional method is to preserve the illustration, isolate added joints and hand variants, drive those parts through the actual existing controller, and verify both rendered poses and the customer flow. Expressions use anticipation, readable holds and a settle. Cases and lettering stay rigid. A gesture's completion cannot overwrite a newer user action or submission state.

The conversion design is a design decision to be measured, not a promise of increased leads. Visitors get clear choices, useful context and a normal link to the demo request. Selecting a mood does not navigate, and no timed performance submits the form. No urgency, price, outcome or testimonial is invented.

The project choice is an allowlisted token, not arbitrary content. Server-rendered and initial hydrated form values agree; the choice is applied once after hydration only if the form remains untouched. Existing validation, field preservation, submission consent and SMTP acceptance semantics remain authoritative.

Primary accessibility guidance checked for this task: [On Input](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/on-input.html) informs predictable control behavior; [Status Messages](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/status-messages.html) informs concise accessible outcomes; [Pause, Stop, Hide](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide.html) informs independent playback control. These references complement the supplied build manual; they do not replace it.
