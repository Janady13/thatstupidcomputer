# Coordinate hostname, URL and content migrations

**Lead:** R25 for release sequencing; R24 for integrated architecture. **Consulted roles:** R01, R02, R03, R08, R09, R11, R12, R16, R17, R20, R21, R24, R25, R26. **Mode:** read-only diagnosis until actual authorization.

## Entry condition

An approved migration changes hosting, hostname, routes, content relationships or identity references. Do not initiate a domain consolidation merely because two brands exist.

## Sequence and ownership

### 1. Inventory the current state

Preserve route/status/canonical maps, important entry points, forms, mail/DNS dependencies, tracking identities and recoverable configuration.

### 2. Design target contracts

R09 establishes page purposes; R08 owns URL/redirect policy; R11 updates entity IDs consistently; R02 reviews hostname/DNS/TLS; R03/R17 preserve inquiry handling.

### 3. Plan rollback and sequence

R24 resolves dependencies. R01 verifies recovery readiness. R25 records exact steps, owners and abort criteria, including forward repair for nonreversible data changes.

### 4. Test before exposure

R21 checks representative redirects, loops, content, forms and analytics on the declared candidate. R20 reviews exposure and sensitive data. No blanket redirect-to-homepage shortcut.

### 5. Accept and monitor

R26 repeats critical public journeys; R16 records measurement annotations; R08/R12 monitor engine outcomes over their actual reporting windows without guaranteeing instant indexing.

## Evidence package

Preserve task/run IDs, exact environment/build, timestamped observations, safe synthetic inputs, expected versus observed state, tool/browser versions, raw private artifacts and separately hashed public-safe redactions. Record unavailable tools and tests not executed. Attach the handoffs rather than compressing distinct observations into one success flag.

## Acceptance

- Approved old-to-new URL contracts are implemented
- Inquiry and identity/measurement dependencies remain valid
- Recovery and postchange critical journeys are observed

## Stop or block

Do not rewrite both brand domains or delete legacy routes without a real inventory and authorization. A sitemap submission cannot substitute for functional migration acceptance.
