## Entity model

Represent Joseph as a Person only with approved factual properties. Represent each actual business/organization distinctly; do not guess whether a domain is a legal entity, brand or trading name. Model each technical article as a document with real headline, author, publisher where appropriate, canonical page and actual dates. `TechArticle` is a vocabulary option, not a promise of a special search feature. [Schema.org Person](https://schema.org/Person), [Organization](https://schema.org/Organization), [TechArticle](https://schema.org/TechArticle).

Stable proposed patterns, **not production IDs**, are `{approved_origin}/about/joseph/#person`, `{approved_origin}/#organization` and `{canonical_article_url}#article`. Do not deploy braces or placeholders. An organization ID may belong on a different approved canonical domain from the person or article; the relationship must reflect the actual publication arrangement.

Only add `sameAs` for verified profiles of that exact entity. Do not use role reference pages, general Wikipedia subjects, programming-language pages or a made-up Wikidata identifier as identity links. Do not mark internal AI personas as authors or employees. Validate visible facts and graph consistency in addition to JSON syntax. [Google structured-data policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/sd-policies).
