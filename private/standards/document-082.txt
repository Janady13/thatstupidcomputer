# Preflight, controlled release and fresh acceptance

**Lead:** R25. **Consulted roles:** R01, R20, R21, R22, R24, R25, R26 plus affected domain owners. **Mode:** read-only diagnosis until actual authorization.

## Entry condition

An actual owner-authorized change has a defined build/environment and predeclared acceptance contract. The library itself performs no deployment.

## Sequence and ownership

### 1. Freeze the contract

Record project/build/environment, exact change scope, required checks, allowed exceptions and required independence. Preserve the contract hash before collecting acceptance records.

### 2. Verify readiness

R01 demonstrates recovery readiness, R21 runs preflight regressions, R22 challenges approved assumptions and R20 reviews critical exposure. R24 resolves conflicts.

### 3. Authorize execution

R25 confirms the actual operator, sequence, stop thresholds and rollback owner. A JSON pass or role prompt cannot grant permission.

### 4. Observe the deployed result

Collect timestamped raw evidence for the actual build. R26 executes fresh required journeys, discloses reviewer relationship and does not accept builder summaries on trust.

### 5. Record the bounded verdict

Validate the record structure and artifact hashes with the offline gate, then have the responsible owner inspect truth and independence. Report failed/blocked requirements, residual risk and actual rollback if performed.

## Evidence package

Preserve task/run IDs, exact environment/build, timestamped observations, safe synthetic inputs, expected versus observed state, tool/browser versions, raw private artifacts and separately hashed public-safe redactions. Record unavailable tools and tests not executed. Attach the handoffs rather than compressing distinct observations into one success flag.

## Acceptance

- Required checks have fresh scoped evidence and match the declared build
- Independence satisfies the agreed policy and is truthfully disclosed
- A responsible owner—not the tool—makes the release decision

## Stop or block

Never create a fake second reviewer identity or modify a failed requirement after testing to obtain a pass. The offline evidence gate checks consistency, not truth or authority.
