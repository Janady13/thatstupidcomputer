# Public expertise layer — briefs and reviewable drafts

This folder is a **publication workbench**, not a ready-to-upload website. It contains 26 distinct role-linked briefs, six substantial methodology drafts and an identity/content plan. None is approved for publication or evidence that a client engagement happened. Do not publish the private library or its site-fact/log templates.

## Use the material

Choose a real buyer problem and an actually demonstrated capability. Review the relevant brief, add safe original evidence where the article makes experience/results claims, obtain Joseph's substantive review and approve a truthful byline. Run claims/privacy and technical publication checks. Choose one canonical host and an actual route before publishing.

Methods articles can explain documented principles without claiming client results. Hypothetical examples must remain labeled. Candidate queries are ideas, not measured search volume. The aim is useful attributable work that may earn discovery and citations, not a guarantee of rankings or recognition.
