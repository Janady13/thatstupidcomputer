# Turn verified work into a public expertise asset

**Lead:** R13. **Consulted roles:** R08, R09, R10, R11, R12, R13, R14, R16, R19, R20, R23, R25, R26. **Mode:** read-only diagnosis until actual authorization.

## Entry condition

A documented method or actual authorized engagement can answer a distinct buyer question. This is a publication workflow, not an instruction to declare expertise without proof.

## Sequence and ownership

### 1. Choose the reader problem

R23 validates audience and demand evidence; R10 judges query relevance; R09 checks existing content so the article adds a distinct answer.

### 2. Collect and classify proof

Separate a sourced methodology article from a real case study. R14 checks exact claims, denominators, dates, credentials and permissions; R20 redacts sensitive artifacts.

### 3. Write and attribute truthfully

R13 builds a useful answer with original explanation and limitations. Joseph must actually review and approve a byline or experience claim. R11 models only verified person/business/document facts.

### 4. Approve a canonical publication

Choose the actual host/route; R08 checks directives and links. R25 obtains owner authorization and controls publication. Do not upload the private library or duplicate the same article across both brands.

### 5. Observe and earn corroboration

R12/R16 measure named-surface citations, referrals and qualified outcomes separately. R19 pursues relevant independent coverage of useful work, disclosing paid/owned relationships.

## Evidence package

Preserve task/run IDs, exact environment/build, timestamped observations, safe synthetic inputs, expected versus observed state, tool/browser versions, raw private artifacts and separately hashed public-safe redactions. Record unavailable tools and tests not executed. Attach the handoffs rather than compressing distinct observations into one success flag.

## Acceptance

- Distinct useful answer and truthful approved authorship
- Material claims have sufficient traceable evidence
- Public artifacts contain no secrets or unauthorized personal data
- Actual publication is owner-authorized and technically checked

## Stop or block

No fabricated results, client logos, certifications, independent reviewers or Wikidata identities. A planning brief and keyword list are not a completed case study or measured demand.
