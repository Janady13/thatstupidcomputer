# From public delivery to useful answers and observed AI citations

**Lead:** R12 for a named AI surface, with R08 owning general crawl/index contracts. **Consulted roles:** R02, R08, R09, R10, R11, R12, R13, R14, R16, R20, R23. **Mode:** read-only diagnosis until actual authorization.

## Entry condition

The business wants relevant search/AI visibility, or a particular named surface does not show its content. No position or citation can be guaranteed by completing the checklist.

## Sequence and ownership

### 1. Name the observation

Record the engine/surface, exact query, locale, timestamp and exposed model state. Different products may not share crawler policies, indexes or reporting.

### 2. Verify delivery and eligibility

R02 establishes public reachability; R08 checks response/directive/canonical/link contracts and available engine reports. R12 checks search/training/user-fetch controls with current provider documentation.

### 3. Evaluate answer value

R10 judges query-to-page relevance; R13 adds distinct useful explanations and genuine proof. R14 approves factual claims. R09 prevents orphan/duplicate pages and R11 keeps identity markup truthful.

### 4. Observe selection separately

R12 records citations and counterexamples with the original source URL and method. Do not infer model training or memory from a citation or a crawl log.

### 5. Reconcile business outcomes

R16 reports available search exposure, referrals and qualified inquiries with compatible scopes. R23 checks whether the questions correspond to real buyer problems.

## Evidence package

Preserve task/run IDs, exact environment/build, timestamped observations, safe synthetic inputs, expected versus observed state, tool/browser versions, raw private artifacts and separately hashed public-safe redactions. Record unavailable tools and tests not executed. Attach the handoffs rather than compressing distinct observations into one success flag.

## Acceptance

- Each asserted outcome has the correct source and observation context
- Crawler access, indexing, citations, referrals and leads are not conflated
- No special-file or formatting guarantee replaces useful content

## Stop or block

Unavailable dashboards or model identifiers remain unavailable. Do not create synthetic testimonials, low-value query-variant pages or guaranteed inclusion promises to fill a measurement gap.
